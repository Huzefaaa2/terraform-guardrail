# Code of Conduct

Terraform Guardrail contributors are expected to keep discussions professional, technical, and
respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, design, documentation, or process.
- Assume good intent, but clarify quickly when something is ambiguous.
- Respect different levels of Terraform, cloud, and security experience.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments; close issues; block users; or escalate to GitHub
support when behavior creates risk for contributors or users.
