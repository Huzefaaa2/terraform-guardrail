TerraGuard Logo Concept

Core idea:
A shielded TG monogram that combines Terraform infrastructure blocks, a guardrail decision loop, and multi-cloud policy nodes.

Symbolism:
- Shield: enterprise governance boundary.
- T: Terraform and infrastructure-as-code building blocks.
- G arc: guardrail loop for policy decisions.
- Four nodes: multi-cloud surfaces and control evidence points.
- Three bottom rails: pass / warn / block.
