# How To Use the v1-v5 Full Platform Streamlit App

Use this app when you want one professional enterprise GUI that demonstrates the full Terraform
Guardrail story across all five releases.

Live app: https://terraform-guardrail-platform.streamlit.app/

## What this app demonstrates

- v1 foundation scanning and optional state leak checks.
- v2 clickable policy catalog, enterprise evaluation, drift, and evidence.
- v3 policy packs.
- v4 explainability, suggested fixes, SARIF, and JUnit.
- v5 remediation, patch bundles, GitHub PR dry runs, schedules, automation, health, and trends.
- Story-driven case-study playbooks for enterprise adoption.

## Step by step

1. Open the v1-v5 Full platform app.
2. Start with **v1 Scan** to upload Terraform files or run the sample scan.
3. Open **v2 Catalog** and click a TG rule ID to read and download the policy snippet.
4. Open **v2-v3 Enterprise**.
5. Pick a policy pack, provider, environment, risk tier, and `fail-on`.
6. Click **Run Full Platform Evaluation**.
7. Open **v4-v5 Governance** to review explainability, remediation, patch previews, PR dry run,
   scheduled scan, evidence schedule, and automation runner output.
8. Open **Evidence + Health** to run a drift check, download evidence, and review health signals.
9. Open **Stories** for practical adoption scenarios and link to the full case-study wiki page.

## Who should use it

- Platform leaders who want a single executive demo.
- Security teams validating guardrail coverage.
- Developers who need to understand findings and fixes.
- Audit and compliance teams that need evidence output.
